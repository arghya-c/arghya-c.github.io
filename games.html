Games

A small collection of games and mathematical curiosities.
Single Player
💡
Lights Out
Toggle lights on a grid where each move affects neighboring cells. Every puzzle is guaranteed to be solvable.
Two Player
⚫
Black Hole
A simple two player game where each player tries to control the final empty spot - the Black Hole!
⭕
Crossing Chords
A geometric two-player game where players connect dots on a circle, creating intersections to score points while planning the perfect matching.
♞
Knight Duel
Two knights roam on a collapsing chessboard where every move burns the square behind them. Trap your opponent or capture their knight before running out of safe moves.
